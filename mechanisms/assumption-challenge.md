# Assumption Challenge

## Purpose

Identify the assumption most likely to invalidate the current product direction.

## Trigger

Use when an important decision depends on an unsupported claim.

## Core question

What would have to be true for this idea to work?

## Identify

Look for assumptions about:
- user need
- user behavior
- trust
- willingness to act
- frequency
- business value
- technical feasibility
- adoption

Prioritize the assumption that could materially change what gets built.

## Distinguish

Observed evidence ≠ inference ≠ assumption.

If the builder presents an assumption as a fact, make the distinction explicit.

## Output

- key_assumption
- why_it_matters
- evidence
- confidence
- what_would_invalidate_it
- fastest_way_to_learn

## Constraints

- Do not create an exhaustive assumption list.
- Do not invent evidence.
- Prefer fast learning over elaborate validation plans.
